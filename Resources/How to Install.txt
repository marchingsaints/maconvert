MacConvert: How to Install
==========================

Needs a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 Sonoma or later.

1. Drag MacConvert onto the Applications folder in this window.

2. Open MacConvert from Applications.
   The first time, macOS will say it can't check the app for malicious
   software, because it isn't signed with an Apple Developer ID yet.
   To allow it once:
     • Click "Done" (or "OK") on that message.
     • Open System Settings › Privacy & Security.
     • Scroll down to the message about MacConvert and click "Open Anyway".
     • Confirm with your password or Touch ID, then click "Open".
   You only need to do this once.

3. In the MacConvert window, click "Turn On" to add Convert, Optimize,
   Compress and Combine to Finder's right-click menu.
   (Or tick MacConvert in System Settings › General › Login Items &
   Extensions › Finder, or › Privacy & Security › Extensions on Sonoma.)

4. Right-click any image, video, audio file, PDF or Office document in Finder.

Tips
• Drop files on the MacConvert icon in the menu bar to convert them.
• Settings (menu bar icon › Settings…) chooses where converted files go.
• Converting Word, Excel or PowerPoint files to PDF uses Microsoft Office,
  or Pages, Keynote or Numbers, if installed.
• After updating MacConvert, relaunch Finder (Option-right-click its Dock
  icon › Relaunch) so the right-click menu picks up the new version.

Everything happens on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded.
